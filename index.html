Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project with us.

How long does a typical project take?
Small MVPs and single-workflow automations usually take 2–5 weeks. Full platforms with AI features or multiple integrations typically run 6–10 weeks. Enterprise systems are scoped individually based on complexity.
How much does a project cost?
Pricing depends on scope — see the packages above as a starting guide. After a free scoping call, we send a fixed quote (not an hourly estimate) so you know the full cost upfront before any work begins.
Do you work with businesses outside Tanzania?
Yes. While we're based in Dar es Salaam and have deep experience with the Tanzanian/East African market (M-Pesa integration, Swahili UI, local compliance), we take on clients regionally and internationally, working fully remote.
What happens after the project launches?
Every package includes a post-launch support window (30–90 days depending on tier) for bug fixes and stabilization. After that, we offer ongoing maintenance retainers or you can take the codebase and run it independently — it's yours.
Can you take over a project another developer left unfinished?
Yes — this is common. We start with a technical audit of whatever exists (code, docs, or none at all), then rebuild or continue from a clean, documented foundation so you're not stuck with someone else's shortcuts.
Do you sign contracts and NDAs?
Yes. Every engagement starts with a written Working Plan and Budget Breakdown, and we're happy to sign an NDA before discussing sensitive project details.
What kind of AI can you build into my product?
From customer-facing chatbots and WhatsApp/Instagram AI assistants to predictive analytics, document processing, and custom LLM-powered agents — we scope the right AI approach for your actual business problem rather than adding AI for its own sake.
